Health, Safety, Security & Environment (HSSE): Safety Rules & Regulations

Through this section, we will introduce our company's in-house safety rules and regulations.

PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)

  • Use the appropriate PPE provided to personnel.
  • To request from Supervisor any other PPE that is required for use in order to carry out work safely.

REPORTING OF UNSAFE CONDITION/ACTS & ACCIDENTS

  • Immediately report any unsafe conditions/acts and accidents to immediate Supervisor.

TRIP AND FALL

  • If you spill your food and drinks, clean it immediately.

HOUSEKEEPING

  • Maintain good housekeeping and the place of work must be kept free of obstruction and from any substance likely to cause slip, trip or fall.

RISK ASSESSMENT (RA)

  • Perform RA for all work activities to be carried out.
  • Use the appropriate hand tools for the work, and all hand tools must be visually checked for any defects before use.

SAFE WORK PROCEDURES (SWP)

  • Comply to all SWP.
  • Inform Management if SWP is not up to date.

SAFETY AWARENESS

  • Participate actively in the safety and health inspections, meeting or discussions.

SAFETY AND WARNING SIGNS

  • Obey safety and warning signs posted.

OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT

  • Must not operate/use machinery and equipment unless authorized.
  • Must possess Certificate of Competency after training.

WORK SAFE BE SAFE

  • Always perform work safely.
  • All works at height of 3m or more are prohibited without the Permit-To-Work (PTW).
  • All works at height below 3m shall comply with the Fall Prevention Plan.
