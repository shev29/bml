Corporate Information: About Us


PT. Berdiri Matahari Logistik (BML) established since 1996 and its mostly owned by LOGISTEED Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd. in Singapore as subsidiary of LOGISTEED, Ltd. in Japan.

BML is known as Total Logistic Service Provider which specialize on 3PL/Contract Logistics, Freight Forwarding, Heavy Transportation, Warehouse and Distribution, Packing, Crating as well as Logistics Consultancy.

BML has grown and evolved broadly its operations network in alignment to Management Principles in order to provide to customers, both locally and globally, an integrated logistics business partnering solutions. BML has been working in close partnership within 27 companies in the Asian Network.

We empower our customers to make dramatic improvements in their logistics management. Our sales, information and engineering capabilities synergize to find the best possible solution to fit each customer's business needs.

Our ability to optimize customers' logistics is based on three strengths:
 - Ability to propose solutions that works well for the customer;
 - Streamline integrated handling of customer's data and engineering technology; and
 - The smooth-running operational efficiency that comes with decades of experience.
